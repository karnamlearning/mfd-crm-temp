# 14 · Meeting detail & outcome

![Meeting detail & outcome](../_shots/14-meeting-detail-light.png)

> **In one line:** One page per meeting: log how it went, keep a voice note, approve an AI summary, see the client's MF Box facts.
>
> **Who uses it:** RM, branch manager, sub-broker (the host); ops staff who get the follow-up tasks · **Open it:** sidebar → Meetings → click a meeting · **Wireframe file:** `14-meeting-detail.html`

## Why this screen matters
Most RMs meet the client, then forget to write anything down. The follow-ups (SIP top-up, nomination form, a statement) get lost. This screen makes logging the outcome a one-minute job right after the meeting, turns what was said into tasks, and puts the client's MF Box numbers next to it so the RM walks in prepared.

## What you see, part by part

### 1. Header
- Meeting title ("Portfolio review – Verma Family") with a status pill: **Outcome pending** (meeting is over but nobody has logged what happened yet).
- Small tags: meeting type, Offline, Andheri office, Today 11:00–11:45 AM, 45 min.
- **Host** (Neha Kapoor) and **Participants** (Rahul Verma, Family Head, and Anjali Verma), with **+ Add** to bring in another client, lead or teammate.
- Buttons on the right: **Reschedule**, a "more" menu (edit, duplicate as next review, copy meeting link, send summary to client, export minutes as PDF, mark as no-show, cancel) and the main button **Mark completed**.

### 2. Log outcome
The card says "takes about a minute" and shows the deadline: **Due today, 6:00 PM**.
- **How did it go?** (required): Positive, Needs follow-up, No interest, Rescheduled. A small purple tag shows "AI suggests: Positive", but you choose.
- **Meeting notes:** free text, where you can @mention (tag) a teammate.
- **Next step, Follow-up date, Follow-up by** (Call, WhatsApp, Visit / meeting, Email). This is what keeps the next action from slipping.
- At the bottom: **Save draft** and **Save outcome**. A note says saving adds the outcome to the meeting's activity feed and to the client's timeline.

### 3. Voice note (with consent)
- You can tap **Record voice note** instead of typing. It works in **Hindi / English**.
- The sample shows a 2:14 clip with a play button and a green **Transcribed** tag (the speech has been turned into text).
- Under it: **"Consent to record: Rahul Verma · 11:03 AM"**. The client's permission to record is captured with the time, so the firm is covered if anyone asks later.

### 4. AI summary (draft), needs your approval
- Purple box drafted from your typed notes plus the voice note. It says plainly: **"Nothing is saved or sent until you approve it."**
- **Summary:** five short bullets (allocation fine, risk profile unchanged at Moderately aggressive, SIP up from ₹10,000 to ₹15,000 from December, Anjali's 2 folios have no nominee and she will sign the form this week, capital-gains statement for FY 2026-27 (year to date) needed before 15 Dec advance tax, insurance not discussed). You can **Edit** or **Regenerate** it.
- **Suggested action items:** three ready-made tasks, each with a type, an owner, a due date and a priority: "Send SIP top-up form – Rahul Verma" (Neha, due 17 Nov, High), "Nomination update for 2 folios – Anjali Verma" (Priya Nair, due 18 Nov) and "Share capital-gains statement" (already sent on WhatsApp at 12:20 PM, so it saves as done). Each has a tick box and an edit pencil.
- You untick what you don't want, then press **Create 3 tasks** (the count changes as you tick). Or press **Discard draft**.
- Footer line: **"Internal — not advice · not shared with the client."**

In short: the AI only drafts. A person reads it, fixes it and approves it. Only then do tasks get created.

### 5. Tabs: Comments & Activities, Tasks, Opportunities, Leads, Attachments

It shows the whole story: meeting created, WhatsApp reminder sent, client confirmed on WhatsApp, prep pack made from MF Box, a teammate's comment, RM checked in at the office, voice note recorded with consent, meeting ended, AI draft awaiting approval, and the capital-gains statement sent to Rahul on WhatsApp at 12:20 PM. You can filter All / Comments / System.
- **Tasks:** tasks linked to this meeting ("Bank mandate renewal – HDFC ••4410", overdue since 13 Nov, and the prep task, done). New tasks from the AI box land here.
- **Opportunities:** "SIP top-up – Rahul Verma", ₹5,000 / month extra (₹10,000 → ₹15,000 from December), stage "Requirements gathered", expected close 30 Nov. The same opportunity shows on Client 360.
- **Leads:** empty, with a nudge: if a family member or referral came up, add them as a lead.
- **Attachments:** the prep pack PDF and a photo of the nomination form; new files go to Documents › Verma Family.

### 7. Linked client

### 9. Reminders
What the system sent or will send: WhatsApp reminder to the client the day before (Sent), in-app reminder 15 minutes before (Sent), **Log outcome prompt** 15 minutes after the meeting on Home and My Day (Active), and a switch to auto-schedule the **next quarterly review** around 14 Feb 2027.

### 10. Reschedule drawer
Opens from the side. Pick a new date and time, mode (Offline / Online / Phone), or one of three **free slots** suggested from your calendar and the client's usual times. Choose a reason, edit the note to participants, and tick "Notify on WhatsApp" and "Log outcome as Rescheduled".

## How to demo it (2 minutes)
1. Open Meetings and click "Portfolio review – Verma Family". Point at **Outcome pending** and **Due today, 6:00 PM**.
2. Click the outcome choices (Positive, Needs follow-up) to show how quick it is.
3. Click **Record voice note**: a red live waveform and timer start. Click **Stop recording**; a message says it is saved and being transcribed. Point at the consent line.
4. Scroll to the purple **AI summary**. Untick one action item: the button changes to "Create 2 tasks". Tick it again and click **Create 3 tasks**. A message confirms the tasks were created and approved by Neha; the Tasks tab count goes up.
5. On the right, walk through the **prep pack**: family AUM, SIP bounce warning, last meeting notes. Mention "Synced from MF Box".
6. Click **Reschedule** to show the drawer with free slots, then Cancel.
7. Click **Mark completed**: the status turns green, "Completed".

## Questions the CEO may ask
**Q: Will the AI create tasks or message clients on its own?**
A: No. It only drafts. The RM edits and approves, and only then are tasks created. The summary is marked internal and is not shared with the client.

**Q: Is recording a client legal?**
A: We record only after the client agrees, and we save who agreed and when ("Consent to record: Rahul Verma · 11:03 AM"). The exact consent wording should be checked by compliance.

**Q: Does the RM have to type in AUM and SIP details?**
A: No. The prep pack is read from MF Box and shows the last sync time. The RM only types what was said in the meeting.

**Q: What if the RM never logs the outcome?**
A: The meeting stays "Outcome pending", a prompt shows on Home and My Day 15 minutes after the meeting, and the outcome has a same-day deadline that managers can see.

**Q: Does it handle Hindi?**
A: The voice note is shown as Hindi / English. How well mixed Hindi-English speech is transcribed needs testing.

## Decisions needed
- **AI summary and voice transcription:** which provider, the cost per meeting, and whether it is part of the base plan or a paid add-on.
- **Recording consent:** how consent is taken (verbal, tap on the RM's phone, WhatsApp "Yes") and how long recordings are kept.
- **Outcome deadline:** is "same day, 6:00 PM" the right rule for every firm, or should each firm set it?
- **"Send summary to client" in the more menu:** allowed or not, given the AI summary is marked "not advice · not shared with the client".
