# 08 · Client 360

![Client 360](../_shots/08-client-360-light.png)

> **In one line:** Everything about one client and their family on one page: money, contacts, tasks, service, documents.
>
> **Who uses it:** RM, sub-broker, branch manager, ops, owner · **Open it:** sidebar → Clients → click a client's name · **Wireframe file:** `08-client-360.html`

## Why this screen matters
Before a call or review, an RM today opens the back office for numbers, WhatsApp for chats, and a notebook or Excel for follow-ups. This page puts all of it together for one client (sample: **Rahul Verma**, Verma Family). Anyone who picks up the client, such as a backup RM, ops or the manager, sees the full story in one minute.

## Questions the CEO may ask
**Q: Can an RM change the AUM or SIP figures here?**
A: No. Money facts are read-only from MF Box. RMs can edit only CRM fields (Key details has an edit pencil) and add notes and activities.

**Q: What is a confidential note?**
A: A note that only the people named on it can read, for example about a family dispute or a health issue. The sample says "Visible only to Neha Kapoor and Rajesh Sharma. You can see it because you are named."

**Q: Will the AI advise the client?**
A: No. Suggestions are internal, marked "not advice", and need a person to act. Nothing is sent to the client automatically.

**Q: Where does the research signal come from?**
A: From AdvisorKhoj's own fund research, matched to what the client holds. It is shown only as a talking point for the review.
