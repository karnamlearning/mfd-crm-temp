The internal CRM for MF Box MFDs, screen by screen

22 clickable screens that keep the layout MFD teams know from the Investwell CRM (as used by DoItNow), redrawn in a minimal blue-and-white system with a full dark theme. The centre of the MVP is daily activity tracking for RMs, branches and sub-brokers.
